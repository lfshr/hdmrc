Our story

Building layouts together for nearly 40 years

The club began in the autumn of 1989, when two members of the Helensburgh Model Boat Builders Club, both Arctic convoy veterans, put an advert in the Helensburgh Advertiser to start a model railway club in the town. Since then our layouts, from Craigendoran Pier to Trenches and Clydemuir, have appeared at exhibitions across Scotland and beyond.
